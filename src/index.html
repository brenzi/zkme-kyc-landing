Kusama Vision applicant verification

Identity and residence verification for the Kusama Vision Program, via zkMe.

What is verified, and nothing else

  • your verified name, date of birth and country (shared with the curators)
  • your country of residence is not embargoed (yes/no only)
  • you are a live person holding your own ID (yes/no only)
  • you have one credential per person (yes/no only)

Your documents and selfie go to zkMe, never to the curators or this page. This page stores nothing, has no backend, and talks only to zkMe. Sanctions screening is done separately by the curators; nothing here replaces it.

How it works

  1. Enter the payout address from your proposal below. It links your verification to your application; use exactly the same address.
  2. Each curator verifies applicants independently, so complete the verification once per curator. The first run takes a few minutes (ID scan and selfie in the zkMe flow); later runs reuse your zkMe credential and take seconds.
  3. When every row shows ✓ verified, tell the curators in your Matrix room.

Each curator sends you a personal verification link in your Matrix room; this page cannot start a verification without one. Opening a link shows that curator's button, with your address and session already filled in; repeat with each curator's link.
